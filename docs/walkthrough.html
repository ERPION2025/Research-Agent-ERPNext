01 · Process

One question, end to end

Three ways in, one loop, three ways out. The loop is the part that matters: it does not stop at the first draft, it scores its own work and re-runs when the score fails. Nothing reaches the ERP without a human in between.

The loop-back on the left is the whole point. Most agents draft, critique and rewrite the prose. This one re-plans and re-queries. If trial one asked the wrong table, no amount of rewriting fixes it. Screen 02 shows exactly that happening.

What is the current COGS on the bike models?
Cmd or Ctrl + Enter to runRun research

Realised COGS is running ₹42,180 per unit on average across the six active models, 6.2% above what the BOMs say it should be.

The gap is concentrated in two models. EM-X1 Pro is 11.4% over BOM and EM-C2 is 9.8% over. The other four sit within 2%.

COGS means three things here and they disagree, which is the finding: stock valuation is what the balance sheet carries, BOM cost is what engineering expects, and realised COGS is what the units we actually sold cost us. On X1 Pro all three agree with each other and all three are above the BOM baseline set in March, so this is real input cost drift, not a valuation artefact.
